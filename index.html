Our Story

Caring Through
Every Meal

For over 15 years, Shankar's Tiffin Service has been a trusted name in providing nutritious, home-style meals to hospital patients across Mumbai. What began as a small family endeavor has grown into a service that touches thousands of lives.

We understand that hospital stays can be challenging. That's why every meal we prepare is crafted with care, following dietary guidelines while never compromising on taste. Our kitchen operates under strict hygiene standards, ensuring safe and healthy food delivery.

Owner Shankar personally oversees each day's preparation, maintaining the quality and warmth that has made us Mumbai's preferred hospital tiffin service.
